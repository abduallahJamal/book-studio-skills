# Six Genre Structural Blueprints (AR & EN)

## 1. `trade-nonfiction` (Idea-Driven Non-Fiction)
- **Arc:** Problem reframing (Ch. 1–2) -> Mechanism & mental models (Ch. 3–6) -> Applied edge cases & failure modes (Ch. 7–8) -> Synthesis & Monday-morning action (Ch. 9–10).
- **Chapter rhythm:** Concrete scene or anomaly -> Conceptual lens -> Counter-example -> Actionable synthesis (never end with "In conclusion...").

## 2. `technical-code` (Engineering & Software Architecture)
- **Arc:** Production failure story & constraints -> Core primitives -> Progressive system build -> Failure modes, observability & benchmarks -> Production checklist.
- **Bilingual Arabic rule:** Keep code identifiers, CLI commands, and architecture acronyms in isolated `LTR` spans/blocks; lock canonical Arabic terms in `editorial/book-bible.md`.

## 3. `literary-novel` (Narrative Fiction & Memoir)
- **Arc:** Inciting disturbance -> Escalating complications -> Midpoint reversal -> High-cost crisis -> Earned resolution.
- **Continuity rule:** Track exact scene timestamps, character knowledge state, and sensory motifs per chapter in `editorial/book-bible.md`.

## 4. `academic-monograph` (Scholarly & Research Monograph)
- **Arc:** Historiography / literature gap -> Theoretical & methodological framework -> Empirical/textual chapters -> Comparative discussion -> Limitations & future inquiry.
- **Apparatus:** Footnote density, formal citations, numbered tables/equations, zero casual contractions.

## 5. `executive-report` (Strategic Playbook & Market Brief)
- **Arc:** Executive thesis & KPI baseline -> Structural drivers -> Comparative matrix -> Risk & mitigation scenarios -> 30/60/90-day execution roadmap.
- **Visual rule:** Every chapter pairs prose with at least one grounded comparison table or vector diagram.

## 6. `classic-arabic-turath` (`الكتاب الأدبي والتراثي العربي`)
- **Arc:** `خطبة الكتاب / المقدمة المنهجية` -> `الأبواب والفصول المقسمة` -> `الفوائد والشواهد والنصوص المحققة` -> `الخاتمة والفهارس العلمية`.
- **Typography:** Partial or full vocalization (`ضبط بالشكل`) on ambiguous words, Quranic/poetic block isolation, rich classical Arabic connectors (`أما بعد`, `ومن لطائف هذا الباب`, `والحاصل أن`) free of modern Western translation calques.
